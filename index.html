What Is SafeCheck?

SafeCheck is a proposed public-record verification platform focused on domestic violence and sexual assault convictions.

Our goal is to make verified public information easier to find, understand, and monitor.

Why SafeCheck?

Verified Information

Designed to connect users with reliable, publicly available conviction records.

Convenient Access

One accessible platform for individuals, parents, and businesses.

Ongoing Updates

Planned alerts when relevant public records are updated.

Public Records Search

Preview of a future SafeCheck feature.

Demonstration only. No live records are searched or stored.

Our Mission

Empowering safer decisions through transparency, accessibility, and awareness.

SafeCheck is a concept under development. Public records may be incomplete or outdated. A record search cannot guarantee personal safety.
